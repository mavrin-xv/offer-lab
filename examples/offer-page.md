# Stock counts that match the shelf on the 1st of every month

For shops with 200+ SKUs on several channels. First match in 7 days.

## Sound familiar?

You run a sale, orders pour in, and on Monday you refund 14 of them because the stock was never there. You fix counts on Sunday nights. Your bookkeeper fixes the books, but the store still shows the wrong number. A sync app helps until a return or a bundle comes back, then it drifts again.

## How it works: the 3-pass count match

1. **Shelf pass.** You send one photo-count of your top 50 SKUs; we take the rest from your last stocktake.
2. **Channel pass.** With read-only access, we match every channel's count to the shelf and fix the gaps.
3. **Returns pass.** We map how returns and bundles move stock, so the counts stay right between months.

You give access once. We send a one-page report of every count we changed and why.

## What you get each month

- The 3-pass count match across all your channels, done by the 1st
- Read-only access setup with a written access policy (we never touch orders or payouts)
- A bundle and returns map, updated when you add products
- A one-page change report your bookkeeper can use as is
