# Scoring rubric

Score each dimension 1 to 5. Write the reason next to the number. If you cannot write the reason, the score is a guess;
give it a 2 and go find the evidence.

## Money: do they pay to fix this today?

| Score | What you saw |
|---|---|
| 5 | They pay a named provider a named amount for this problem now, and complain about the result |
| 4 | They pay for something close (a bookkeeper who does part of it, an app that half solves it) |
| 3 | They spend their own time on it every week or month and can say how much |
| 2 | They agree it costs them, but cannot name what they spend |
| 1 | No spend, no budget line, "we just live with it" with no sign of cost |

Current spend beats market size. A market report saying the category is large tells you nothing about whether this
buyer will pay you.

## Channel: can you reach them by name?

| Score | What you saw |
|---|---|
| 5 | A list you can download or build (a directory, a marketplace seller list) plus a community where they ask for help |
| 4 | Two or more exact search queries or groups where they post about this problem |
| 3 | One community or query, active in the last 90 days |
| 2 | "They are on social media" or "they go to conferences": no exact place |
| 1 | No idea where they gather |

Cap: no exact query, list or community written down means 2 at most.

## Pain: do they name it without you?

| Score | What you saw |
|---|---|
| 5 | Their exact words, unprompted, in 3 or more places, with a cost attached ("refunded 14 orders") |
| 4 | Their words in 3 or more places, no cost attached |
| 3 | You described it and they agreed, or fewer than 3 instances |
| 2 | They agree when asked, then change the subject |
| 1 | They do not see it as a problem |

Caps: no quote in their words means 3 at most; fewer than 3 named instances (people, posts, threads) means 3 at most.

## Thresholds

SHIP 11 or more, ITERATE 8 to 10, KILL under 8. A 1 anywhere holds the niche at ITERATE.

These thresholds are this pack's defaults, not a published standard. Change them in your copy of the script if your
evidence says otherwise, and write down why.
