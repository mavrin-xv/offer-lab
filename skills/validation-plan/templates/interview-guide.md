## Opening (1 minute)
"I am looking into how <buyer type> deal with <problem area>. I am not selling anything today. Can I ask about the
last time it came up?"

## Past, specific (15 minutes)
- Tell me about the last time <problem> happened. What happened?
- What did you do about it?
- What did it cost: time, money, refunds, a sale?
- What have you tried before? Why did you stop?
- How do you handle it today? Can you show me?

## Switch questions (if they changed something recently)
- What made the old way stop working? (push)
- What did you hope the new way would do? (pull)
- What almost stopped you? (anxiety)
- What do you miss about the old way? (habit)

## Ask for a commitment (2 minutes)
Pick one: a second call with a screen share, an introduction to a peer, access to a sample of their data, a
pre-order at the stated price.
